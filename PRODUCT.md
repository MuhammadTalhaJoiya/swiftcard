# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two distinct audiences:

**Shoppers** — consumers browsing a curated product catalog, adding to cart, and completing orders. They arrive with intent and want frictionless discovery-to-checkout. Context: desktop and mobile, short sessions, task-focused.

**Admin** — a single operator managing the store: adding/editing products, monitoring orders, viewing analytics, and using AI tools. Context: desktop, longer sessions, data-heavy workflow.

## Product Purpose

SwiftCard is a full-featured ecommerce platform — product catalog, cart, checkout, order management, admin dashboard, analytics, and AI-powered tools (chatbot, product descriptions, invoice OCR). Success means both audiences accomplish their core jobs without friction or confusion.

## Brand Personality

Bold, modern, energetic. Confident without being loud. Clear visual hierarchy that commands attention. The kind of product that feels like it was built by people who care about craft.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap/MUI SaaS: avoid blue-button defaults, identical card grids, generic dashboard templates, flat uninspired typography
- Cluttered marketplace aesthetics (eBay, Amazon): no banner overload, no rainbow color usage
- Dated ecommerce templates: no skeuomorphic shadows, no stock-photo hero banners

## Design Principles

1. **Hierarchy earns trust** — every screen has one clear focal point; secondary information steps back without disappearing
2. **Speed is a feature** — interactions feel instant; loading states are minimal and non-disruptive
3. **Admin and shopper deserve equal craft** — the dashboard is not an afterthought; it gets the same visual attention as the storefront
4. **Confident defaults** — design makes clear choices rather than hedging; no wishy-washy neutral layouts
5. **Data should be readable, not decorative** — analytics and order tables prioritize legibility over chart ornamentation

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Adequate color contrast on all interactive elements. Focus states visible. No color-only information encoding.
